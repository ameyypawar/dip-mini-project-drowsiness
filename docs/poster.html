Pipeline
01
Enhancement
CLAHE (clip 2.0, tile 4×4), gamma 1.5, glare inpainting. Normalised to 64×64.
02
Filtering
Median 3×3 for impulse noise, mean 5×5 for Gaussian. Chosen by PSNR/SSIM.
03
Segmentation
Otsu thresholding and seeded region growing to isolate the pupil.
04
Morphology
Opening 3×3, closing 5×5, hole-fill. 14 region-shape features.
05
Features
HOG + LBP texture descriptors, 1774 dimensions, RBF-SVM classifier.
06
Temporal
PERCLOS over a 10 s window with hysteresis to suppress flicker.
Results
What the measurements showed

Texture outperformed shape

Segmentation and transform features carried real signal alone (0.630 and 0.660) but proved redundant once texture descriptors were present — a measured result, not an assumption.

Quality metrics mislead

At JPEG Q15, SSIM reads 0.91 while classification has already lost 8.4 points. Optimising a vision pipeline for PSNR or SSIM optimises the wrong objective.

Denoising rescues segmentation

Salt-and-pepper noise drove Otsu mask IoU to 0.000 on a poorly-lit sample; a median 3×3 filter beforehand restored it to 0.90–0.98.
