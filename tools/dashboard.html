Reading the stages

Watch the contrast figure jump between grayscale and equalised, then watch the median filter pull the noise figure back down. Raise the morphology slider and the segmented region loses its speckle.

What this is not

These are the image-processing stages only. The eye-state decision comes from a support vector machine trained on 1,774 HOG and LBP features, which runs in Python and scores 89.65% on held-out subjects.

Evidence

What the measurements showed

Texture outperformed shape

Segmentation and transform features carried real signal alone, 0.630 and 0.660, but proved redundant once texture descriptors were present. Measured, not assumed.

Quality metrics mislead

At JPEG quality 15, SSIM still reads 0.91 while classification has already lost 8.4 points. Tuning a vision pipeline for PSNR or SSIM tunes the wrong thing.

Denoising rescues segmentation

Salt-and-pepper noise drove Otsu mask IoU to 0.000 on a poorly lit sample. A median 3×3 filter beforehand brought it back to 0.90–0.98.

Limitations

  • Alert timing is the weak point. Per-frame accuracy is 0.903, but alert-timing agreement is 0.711. Frame-level noise accumulates across the 10-second PERCLOS window.
  • No real driving footage. The temporal stage is validated on a synthetic sequence assembled from dataset stills. The webcam path is written but untested.
  • Adaptive EAR threshold is unproven. Showing it beats a fixed threshold needs multi-subject video that this dataset does not contain.
  • Infrared eye crops only. No full-face driving data, so face localisation is exercised only on the inference path, never on the training set.
